Case Study:
A 72-year-old female is admitted with severe community-acquired pneumonia. Arterial blood gas shows: pH 7.28, PaCO2 56 mmHg, PaO2 58 mmHg, HCO3 26 mEq/L on 4 L/min nasal cannula.

Which of the following acid-base disturbances is demonstrated by these laboratory values?
A. Uncompensated metabolic acidosis
B. Respiratory acidosis with hypoxemia
C. Fully compensated metabolic alkalosis
D. Respiratory alkalosis
